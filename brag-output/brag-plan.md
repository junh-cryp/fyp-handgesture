# Brag Plan: BimTalk

## What is this app?

BimTalk is an offline-first Flutter communication app that turns selected Bahasa Isyarat Malaysia (BIM) signs into confirmed text and spoken Malay, with learning and quick-phrase tools alongside it.

## The angle

Communication should not disappear when the connection does. BimTalk’s story is a deliberate, human-centred loop: show a sign, let the app see the hand and body landmarks, let the user confirm the intended word, then let the phone speak. The video treats that moment with the calm confidence of a polished app-store launch, not a vague “AI” demo.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

“When words need a hand.” A single raised hand appears inside a phone frame; its landmark skeleton resolves into BimTalk’s indigo visual system.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Live camera recognition: hand and pose landmarks appear over a real camera-style screen.
- Human-in-the-loop selection: a “BAGUS” candidate card is deliberately tapped before text becomes speech.
- Communication beyond the camera: saved favourite phrases and a 3D BIM lesson avatar make the app useful before and after a sign is recognised.

## Outro / punchline

“BimTalk — see the sign. Choose the word. Let it speak.”

## User flow worth showing

Open Translate → perform a BIM sign in the live camera view → review and confirm a candidate → hear the selected Malay word. Supporting beats: type or select a favourite phrase in Speak, then open a 3D sign lesson.

## Tone

- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: calm, accessible Malaysian mobile-app launch
- Interpretation: clean feature cards, restrained motion, generous readability, and warm utility rather than hype.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

- Display font: system sans-serif / Flutter Material fallback
- Body font: system sans-serif / Flutter Material fallback
- Strongest visual element: BimTalk’s indigo landmark overlay on the live camera preview, followed by the candidate selection card.

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction

- Role: warm, polished mobile-product bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, steady and clean
- Music treatment: begin at 0s around 0.34 volume; lightly fade under the final lockup.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-12 cue preset, ~109.96 BPM. Major visual locks at 8.74s (candidate confirmation) and 17.47s (avatar reveal); menu cards enter around the 13.11s beat window and hold for readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; make the indigo phone glow and final logo halo breathe gently with music energy. No waveform or equalizer graphics.
- SFX posture: restrained, low/medium high-frequency-risk UI clicks and soft reveal accents only.
- Audio-coupled moments: hand-landmark resolve; candidate tap; favourite-phrase card arrival; final BimTalk lockup.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no aggressive impacts, and never let sound obscure the communication-first message.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — A hand becomes a signal — 0.00–3.00s

A warm beige background resolves into a floating phone. A hand silhouette becomes 21 landmark dots and connecting lines. Text: “When words need a hand.”

Sequential/interaction: none.

Audio intent: soft arrival, then a confident bed.

Audio-coupled idea: one gentle reveal accent as the landmarks lock.

Music: vol-12 starts at 0s.

Transition mood: smooth indigo wipe → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — See the sign — 3.00–8.74s

Recreate BimTalk Live: a camera-style phone screen, hand skeleton and body reference dots, and the label “On-device landmark detection.” A compact flow marker reads “Hand + body landmarks”.

Sequential/interaction: yes — hand landmarks settle first, then body reference points, then the feature label; hold all three together.

Audio intent: clear, reassuring momentum.

Audio-coupled idea: subtle soft-drop accents for the two feature labels.

Music: steady; keep text readable despite beat timing.

Transition mood: clean card lift → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Choose the word — 8.74–13.11s

At the 8.74s cue, show a real candidate-style UI card: “Confirm Detected Sign”, candidate “BAGUS”, and a visible tap. The resulting word moves into a speech bubble with a speaker icon. Text: “You stay in control.”

Sequential/interaction: yes — candidate card appears, simulated tap selects BAGUS, then its speech bubble appears; each result remains visible long enough to read.

Audio intent: satisfying confirmation, never alarm-like.

Audio-coupled idea: selection click at the simulated tap, soft success accent on the speech bubble.

Music: lock the candidate-card payoff to the 8.74s strong cue.

Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Say it your way — 13.11–17.47s

Show the Speak screen as an actual app-style composition: typed Malay text, a green Speak button, and two favourite phrase chips arriving one by one. Small text: “Type. Save. Speak.”

Sequential/interaction: yes — phrase chips arrive around the 13.11s and 14.20s beat windows, then the speak button pulses once; hold the full group.

Audio intent: friendly everyday usefulness.

Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click on the Speak button; no repeated typing sounds.

Music: steady, with a gentle lift into the avatar reveal.

Transition mood: soft dimensional rise → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Learn, then connect — 17.47–22.00s

At the 17.47s cue, show BimTalk’s 3D lesson view with a BIM avatar and a sign-selection tray. The final lockup shows the real logo asset and the line: “See the sign. Choose the word. Let it speak.”

Sequential/interaction: yes — avatar appears, a lesson chip selects “BAGUS”, then the logo and final line settle together.

Audio intent: warm resolution.

Audio-coupled idea: one low-key success accent on the final lockup.

Music: fade gently from 21.2s to 22.0s.

Transition mood: hold on final poster frame.
